# Member Communication & Broadcasts

Learn how to effectively communicate with your chapter members through GreekDash's comprehensive communication tools.

## Overview

GreekDash provides multiple communication channels to keep your chapter connected:
- **Broadcast Messages**: Send announcements to all or selected members
- **Individual Messaging**: Direct communication with specific members
- **Automated Notifications**: System-generated updates and reminders
- **Email Campaigns**: Structured email communications
- **SMS Notifications**: Text message alerts for urgent communications

## Accessing Communication Tools

### From the Admin Dashboard

1. **Navigate to "Management" in the sidebar**
2. **Click "Broadcasts"** for group communications
3. **Or access individual messaging** through member profiles

### From Member Directory

1. **Go to Members > Member List**
2. **Select members** for targeted communication
3. **Use "Communicate" button** for bulk messaging

## Broadcast Messages

### Creating a Broadcast

**Step 1: Start New Broadcast**
1. Click "Create Broadcast" button
2. Choose broadcast type (Email, SMS, or Both)
3. Select your target audience

**Step 2: Audience Selection**
- **All Members**: Send to entire chapter
- **By Role**: Target specific roles (Members, Admins, Owners)
- **By Committee**: Send to specific committees or groups
- **Custom Selection**: Choose individual members
- **By Status**: Active, inactive, or pending members

**Step 3: Compose Message**
- **Subject Line**: Clear, descriptive subject (for emails)
- **Message Content**: Your announcement or information
- **Formatting Options**: Bold, italic, links, and basic formatting
- **Attachments**: Add files or documents (email only)

**Step 4: Schedule and Send**
- **Send Immediately**: Deliver right away
- **Schedule for Later**: Set specific date and time
- **Save as Draft**: Complete later
- **Preview**: Review before sending

### Broadcast Types

**Announcements**
- Chapter news and updates
- Policy changes
- Important deadlines
- Event announcements

**Reminders**
- Upcoming events
- Payment due dates
- Meeting notifications
- Deadline reminders

**Emergency Communications**
- Urgent chapter matters
- Safety notifications
- Last-minute changes
- Crisis communications

### Message Templates

**Creating Templates**
1. Go to Communication Settings
2. Click "Create Template"
3. Design your template with placeholders
4. Save for future use

**Template Variables**
- `{member_name}`: Personalizes with member's name
- `{chapter_name}`: Inserts chapter name
- `{event_name}`: For event-related communications
- `{due_date}`: For payment or deadline reminders

**Pre-built Templates**
- Welcome messages for new members
- Event RSVP reminders
- Payment due notifications
- Meeting announcements
- Holiday greetings
