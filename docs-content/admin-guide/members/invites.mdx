# Inviting Members

Learn how to invite new members to your chapter, manage invitations, and ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## Accessing the Invites Section

1. **Navigate to the admin dashboard**
2. **Click on "Members" in the sidebar**
3. **Select "Invites" from the submenu**

You'll see the invites management interface with options to send new invitations and track existing ones.

## Sending Individual Invitations

### Step 1: Start a New Invitation

1. **Click the "Invite Member" button**
2. **Enter the member's email address**
3. **Select the appropriate role** (Member, Admin, or Owner)
4. **Add a personal message** (optional but recommended)

### Step 2: Configure Invitation Settings

**Role Selection Guidelines:**
- **Member**: Standard chapter member access
- **Admin**: Administrative privileges for chapter management
- **Owner**: Full access including billing (use sparingly)

**Personal Message Tips:**
- Welcome the new member warmly
- Explain what GreekDash is and why you're using it
- Provide any chapter-specific context
- Include your contact information for questions

### Step 3: Send the Invitation

1. **Review all information** for accuracy
2. **Click "Send Invitation"**
3. **Confirm the invitation** was sent successfully

The system will display a confirmation message and add the invitation to your tracking list.

## Bulk Invitations

For inviting multiple members at once:

### Preparing Your Member List

Create a CSV file with the following columns:
- `email` (required): Member's email address
- `role` (optional): Member, Admin, or Owner (defaults to Member)
- `first_name` (optional): Member's first name
- `last_name` (optional): Member's last name

**Example CSV format:**
```csv
email,role,first_name,last_name
john.doe@university.edu,Member,John,Doe
jane.smith@university.edu,Admin,Jane,Smith
```

### Uploading Bulk Invitations

1. **Click "Bulk Invite" button**
2. **Upload your CSV file**
3. **Review the preview** of members to be invited
4. **Add a group message** (optional)
5. **Click "Send All Invitations"**

The system will process each invitation and provide a summary of successful and failed invitations.

## Managing Existing Invitations

### Invitation Status Types

**Pending**: Invitation sent but not yet accepted
**Accepted**: Member has created account and joined
**Expired**: Invitation has passed expiration date
**Failed**: Invitation could not be delivered

### Tracking Invitations

The invitations list shows:
- Member email address
- Invitation status
- Date sent
- Assigned role
- Actions available

### Available Actions

**Resend Invitation**
- Use for expired or failed invitations
- Updates expiration date
- Sends fresh invitation email

**Cancel Invitation**
- Removes pending invitation
- Prevents future acceptance
- Use for incorrect invitations

**Change Role**
- Modify role before acceptance
- Useful for correcting mistakes
- Takes effect when member joins

## Troubleshooting Invitations

### Common Issues and Solutions

**Member didn't receive invitation**
1. Check email address for typos
2. Ask member to check spam/junk folder
3. Verify email server isn't blocking invitations
4. Resend invitation if necessary

**Invitation link doesn't work**
1. Check if invitation has expired
2. Ensure link wasn't modified by email client
3. Resend fresh invitation
4. Contact support if issue persists

**Member can't complete registration**
1. Verify invitation is still pending
2. Check for account conflicts (existing email)
3. Ensure member is using correct email
4. Guide member through registration process

### Invitation Limits

Different plans have different invitation limits:
- **Free Plan**: 50 active members
- **Basic Plan**: 200 active members
- **Pro Plan**: 500 active members
- **Enterprise**: Unlimited members

## Best Practices

### Before Sending Invitations

- **Verify email addresses** are correct and current
- **Choose appropriate roles** based on member responsibilities
- **Prepare welcome materials** for new members
- **Coordinate with other admins** to avoid duplicate invitations

### Writing Effective Invitation Messages

- **Be welcoming and enthusiastic**
- **Explain the purpose** of using GreekDash
- **Provide context** about your chapter's adoption
- **Include contact information** for questions
- **Set expectations** for next steps

### After Sending Invitations

- **Monitor invitation status** regularly
- **Follow up** with members who haven't responded
- **Provide support** during the registration process
- **Welcome new members** once they join

### Automation Options

## Security Considerations

### Role Assignment Security

- **Limit Owner roles** to essential personnel only
- **Review Admin assignments** regularly
- **Use Member role** as default for most invitations
- **Document role decisions** for audit purposes

### Invitation Security

- **Monitor invitation activity** through audit logs
- **Set appropriate expiration times** for invitations
- **Revoke unused invitations** promptly
- **Verify member identity** before approving sensitive roles
