# RSVP Management

Master the art of managing event RSVPs, tracking attendance, and optimizing member participation with GreekDash's comprehensive RSVP tools.

## Understanding RSVP System

### RSVP Response Types
- **Yes**: Member confirms attendance
- **No**: Member declines to attend
- **Maybe**: Member is uncertain about attendance
- **No Response**: Member hasn't responded to the invitation

### RSVP Status Tracking
- **Confirmed**: Member has RSVP'd yes and is expected to attend
- **Waitlisted**: Member wants to attend but event is at capacity
- **Declined**: Member has indicated they cannot attend
- **Pending**: Invitation sent but no response received

## Waitlist Management

### How Waitlists Work
1. Event reaches maximum capacity
2. Additional RSVPs are automatically waitlisted
3. Members receive waitlist confirmation
4. Admins can promote waitlisted members when spots open
5. Automatic notifications sent when promoted

## RSVP Communication and Reminders

### Automatic Reminder Schedule
- **Initial Invitation**: Sent when event is published
- **First Reminder**: 1 week before RSVP deadline
- **Final Reminder**: 24 hours before RSVP deadline
- **Confirmation**: 24 hours before event start time

## Guest Management

### Guest RSVP Process
1. Member indicates they're bringing guests during RSVP
2. Guest information collected (name, contact, dietary restrictions)
3. Guest count applied to event capacity
4. Guest notifications sent if configured

### Guest Policies and Settings
- **Guest Limit**: Maximum guests per member
- **Guest Approval**: Require admin approval for guests
- **Guest Fees**: Additional costs for guest attendance
- **Guest Information**: Required details for planning

### Managing Guest Lists
- **View All Guests**: Complete list of expected guests
- **Guest Check-in**: Separate check-in process for guests
- **Guest Communications**: Send information to guests
- **Guest Restrictions**: Manage guest access and permissions

## RSVP Analytics and Reporting

### Key RSVP Metrics
- **Response Rate**: Percentage of invited members who responded
- **Confirmation Rate**: Percentage who RSVP'd yes
- **Waitlist Conversion**: Waitlisted members who got promoted and attended
- **Last-minute Changes**: RSVPs changed within 24 hours of event

### RSVP Timeline Analysis
Track how RSVPs come in over time to optimize future event planning:
- **Early Birds**: Members who RSVP immediately
- **Procrastinators**: Members who wait until deadline
- **Peak Response Times**: When most RSVPs are submitted
- **Reminder Effectiveness**: Response rates after reminders

### Reporting Features
- **RSVP Summary Report**: Complete event RSVP overview
- **Attendee List Export**: Downloadable attendee information
- **Dietary Restrictions**: Summary of special requirements
- **Contact Information**: Emergency contacts and phone numbers

## Best Practices for RSVP Management

### Setting Realistic Expectations
- **Historical Data**: Use past event data to predict attendance
- **Seasonal Patterns**: Account for academic calendar and holidays
- **Member Preferences**: Consider member feedback and preferences
- **Venue Constraints**: Always respect venue capacity limits

### Communication Strategy
- **Clear Deadlines**: Set and communicate firm RSVP deadlines
- **Multiple Reminders**: Send strategic reminders without overwhelming
- **Personal Touch**: Include personal messages from leadership
- **Easy Process**: Make RSVP process simple and accessible

### Waitlist Management
- **Transparent Communication**: Keep waitlisted members informed
- **Fair Process**: Use first-come, first-served for promotions
- **Alternative Options**: Offer alternatives for waitlisted members
- **Future Planning**: Use waitlist data for future event sizing

## Troubleshooting RSVP Issues

### Common Problems and Solutions

#### Low Response Rates
- **Check timing**: Avoid busy academic periods
- **Improve messaging**: Make invitations more compelling
- **Simplify process**: Reduce barriers to RSVP
- **Follow up personally**: Have officers reach out individually

#### Technical Issues
- **RSVP not working**: Check member permissions and email delivery
- **Duplicate responses**: Verify system settings and member accounts
- **Notification failures**: Review communication preferences
- **Capacity errors**: Check event settings and member eligibility

#### Last-minute Changes
- **Cancellation policy**: Establish clear cancellation procedures
- **Waitlist promotion**: Have system for quick waitlist management
- **Communication plan**: Prepare templates for urgent updates
- **Backup planning**: Always have contingency plans ready
