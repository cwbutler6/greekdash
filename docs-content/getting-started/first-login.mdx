# First Login and Setup

Welcome to GreekDash! This guide will walk you through your first login and the essential setup steps to get your chapter up and running.

## Before You Begin

Make sure you have:
- Received your invitation email from GreekDash
- Access to your chapter's email account
- Basic chapter information ready (name, colors, logo, etc.)

## Step 1: Accept Your Invitation

1. **Check your email** for the GreekDash invitation
2. **Click the invitation link** in the email
3. **Create your password** when prompted
4. **Verify your email address** if required

> **Tip**: If you don't see the invitation email, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Step 2: Complete Your Profile

After logging in for the first time:

1. **Upload a profile photo** (optional but recommended)
2. **Add your phone number** for SMS notifications
3. **Set your notification preferences**
4. **Complete your member profile information**

## Step 3: Chapter Setup Wizard

GreekDash will guide you through the essential chapter setup:

### Basic Information
- Chapter name and Greek letters
- Chapter type (fraternity/sorority)
- University/college affiliation
- Chapter founding date

### Branding
- Upload your chapter logo
- Set primary and secondary colors
- Choose your chapter's public URL slug

### Initial Settings
- Set your timezone
- Configure privacy settings
- Choose default notification preferences

## Step 4: Invite Your First Members

Once your chapter is set up:

1. **Go to Members > Invites**
2. **Add member email addresses**
3. **Assign appropriate roles** (Member, Admin, Owner)
4. **Send invitations**

### Role Guidelines
- **Owner**: Full access, billing management (limit 1-2 people)
- **Admin**: Chapter management, member operations
- **Member**: Basic portal access

## Step 5: Configure Essential Features

### Financial Setup (if applicable)
- Connect your Stripe account for dues collection
- Set up your first dues plan
- Configure payment methods

### Communication Setup
- Set up SMS notifications (optional)
- Configure email templates
- Set broadcast preferences

## Next Steps

After completing the initial setup:

1. **Explore the admin dashboard** to familiarize yourself with features
2. **Review the [Admin Guide](/docs/admin-guide)** for detailed feature documentation
3. **Set up your first event** to test the system
4. **Import existing member data** if migrating from another system

## Common First-Time Issues

### Can't Access Admin Features
- Verify your role is set to Admin or Owner
- Contact the person who invited you to check your permissions

### Email Invitations Not Working
- Check that email addresses are correct
- Verify your email sending limits haven't been reached
- Contact support if issues persist

### Stripe Connection Issues
- Ensure you have a verified Stripe account
- Check that your Stripe account supports your country
- Review Stripe's requirements for non-profit organizations

## Getting Help

If you need assistance during setup:

- **Use the help widget** in the bottom-right corner
- **Check our [FAQ section](/docs/admin-guide/faq)**
- **Contact support** through the admin dashboard
- **Schedule a demo** for personalized assistance
