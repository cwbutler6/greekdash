# Member FAQ - Common Questions

## Getting Started

**Q: How do I join my chapter on GreekDash?**
A: You can join either through an invitation email from an admin or by using your chapter's join code on the chapter page.

**Q: Why does my account say "Pending"?**
A: Your membership is awaiting approval from a chapter admin. This is normal for new members joining via join code.

**Q: How long does approval take?**
A: Most chapters approve new members within 24-48 hours. Contact your chapter admin if it's been longer.

## Profile & Account

**Q: How do I update my profile information?**
A: Go to your profile settings and update any information. Changes are saved automatically.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Yes, update your email in Profile Settings. You'll need to verify the new email address.

**Q: Who can see my profile information?**
A: Only members of your chapter and chapter admins can view your profile.

## Payments & Dues

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover) and bank transfers through Stripe.

**Q: Is it safe to enter my payment information?**
A: Yes, all payments are processed securely through Stripe, which uses bank-level security.

**Q: When are dues typically due?**
A: Due dates vary by chapter. Check your dashboard for specific due dates and amounts.

**Q: Can I set up automatic payments?**
A: This depends on your chapter's settings. Contact your admin about payment plan options.

**Q: What if I can't afford my dues right now?**
A: Contact your chapter admin to discuss payment plans or financial assistance options.

**Q: How do I get a receipt for my payment?**
A: Receipts are automatically emailed after payment and available in your payment history.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refund policies vary by chapter. Contact your chapter admin for specific policies.

## Events & Activities

**Q: How do I RSVP for events?**
A: Click on any event in your dashboard and select your attendance status.

**Q: Can I change my RSVP after submitting it?**
A: Yes, you can update your RSVP anytime before the event deadline.

**Q: Can I bring guests to events?**
A: Guest policies vary by event. Check the event details or contact your chapter admin.

**Q: What if I need to cancel last minute?**
A: Update your RSVP as soon as possible and contact the event organizer if it's very last minute.

## Communication & Notifications

**Q: Why am I not receiving email notifications?**
A: Check your spam folder and verify your email preferences in profile settings.

**Q: Can I unsubscribe from emails?**
A: You can unsubscribe from marketing emails, but important transactional emails (like receipts) cannot be disabled.

**Q: How do I update my notification preferences?**
A: Go to Profile Settings → Notifications to customize what emails you receive.

## Technical Issues

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your password.

**Q: The website isn't working properly on my phone.**
A: Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. GreekDash is mobile-friendly and should work on all devices.

**Q: I'm having trouble logging in.**
A: Make sure you're using the correct email and password. Try resetting your password if needed.

## Privacy & Security

**Q: How is my personal information protected?**
A: We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Read our privacy policy for full details.

**Q: Can I control what information other members see?**
A: Yes, you can adjust privacy settings in your profile to control what information is visible.

**Q: What happens to my data if I leave the chapter?**
A: Contact your chapter admin about account deactivation. They can help with data removal if needed.

## Getting Help

**Q: Who should I contact for help?**
A: For chapter-specific questions, contact your chapter admin first. For technical issues, email support@greekdash.com.

**Q: How quickly will I get a response to support requests?**
A: We typically respond to support requests within 24 hours during business days.

**Q: Is there a phone number I can call?**
A: Currently, we provide support via email. This allows us to better track and resolve issues.
