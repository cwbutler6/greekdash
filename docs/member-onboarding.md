## Getting Started

### How to Join
There are two ways to join your chapter on GreekDash:

**Option 1: Invitation Link**
- You'll receive an email invitation from a chapter admin
- Click the secure link in the email
- Complete your profile setup
- Wait for admin approval (if required)

**Option 2: Join Code**
- Get the chapter join code from an admin or current member
- Visit your chapter's page
- Enter the join code
- Complete your profile setup
- Wait for admin approval

### Account Status
After joining, you may see different statuses:
- **Pending**: Waiting for admin approval
- **Active Member**: Full access to chapter portal
- **Admin**: Additional management privileges

## Dues & Payments

### Understanding Dues
Your chapter may have different types of dues:
- **Membership Dues**: Regular chapter fees
- **Event Fees**: Specific event costs
- **Special Assessments**: One-time charges

### Making Payments
1. **View Outstanding Balances**:
   - Check your dashboard for due amounts
   - See payment history and receipts

2. **Payment Process**:
   - Click "Pay Now" on any outstanding balance
   - Secure payment via Stripe (credit card or bank)
   - Receive immediate email confirmation

3. **Payment Plans**:
   - Some chapters offer installment options
   - Set up automatic payments if available

### Payment Receipts
- All payments generate automatic receipts
- Access receipts anytime in your payment history
- Download for tax or reimbursement purposes

## Events & RSVPs

### Viewing Events
- See all upcoming chapter events on your dashboard
- Filter by event type or date
- View event details, location, and requirements

### RSVP Process
1. **Respond to Events**:
   - Click on any event to see details
   - Select "Attending," "Not Attending," or "Maybe"
   - Add any required information (dietary restrictions, etc.)

2. **Event Reminders**:
   - Receive automatic reminders before events
   - Update your RSVP anytime before the deadline

3. **Event Check-in**:
   - Some events may require check-in upon arrival
   - Show your member portal or confirmation email

## Communication

### Receiving Updates
Stay informed through multiple channels:
- **Dashboard Announcements**: Important chapter news
- **Email Notifications**: Event reminders and updates
- **SMS Alerts**: Urgent notifications (if enabled)

### Communication Preferences
Customize how you receive information:
- Choose notification types (events, announcements, dues)

### Unsubscribe Options
- You can unsubscribe from marketing emails
- Important transactional emails (receipts, dues) cannot be disabled
- Contact admins if you need to update communication preferences

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Account & Access
**Q: I can't log in to my account**
A: Try resetting your password. If you're still having trouble, contact your chapter admin.

**Q: My account shows "Pending" status**
A: Your membership is awaiting admin approval. Contact your chapter admin for status updates.

**Q: I want to update my email address**
A: Go to Profile Settings → Contact Information to update your email.

### Payments & Dues
**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: We accept all major credit cards and bank transfers through Stripe.

**Q: Can I get a refund for dues?**
A: Refund policies vary by chapter. Contact your chapter admin for specific policies.

**Q: I didn't receive a payment receipt**
A: Check your spam folder first. You can also download receipts from your payment history.

**Q: Can I set up automatic payments?**
A: This depends on your chapter's settings. Check with your admin about payment plan options.

### Events & Activities
**Q: How do I RSVP for events?**
A: Click on any event in your dashboard and select your attendance status.

**Q: Can I bring guests to events?**
A: Guest policies vary by event and chapter. Check event details or contact admins.

**Q: I need to change my RSVP**
A: You can update your RSVP anytime before the event deadline.

### Privacy & Data
**Q: Who can see my profile information?**
A: Only chapter members and admins can see your profile. You control what information is visible.

**Q: How is my data protected?**
A: We use industry-standard security measures. Read our privacy policy for full details.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Contact your chapter admin to discuss account deactivation or data removal.

### Technical Issues
**Q: The website isn't working properly**
A: Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. Contact support if issues persist.

**Q: I'm not receiving email notifications**
A: Check your spam folder and email preferences in your profile settings.
