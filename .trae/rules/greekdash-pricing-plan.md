| Plan | Price | Who It’s For | Key Features | Limits / Support |
|------|-------|--------------|--------------|------------------|

## Why This Works for You (Solo Dev)
- Support load is capped by member limits + tiered support.
- Clear jump in value at Pro; add-ons avoid feature bloat in Basic.
- 2 months free incentive smooths MRR and reduces churn.
- Enterprise tier keeps doors open for $5k–$50k annual deals.

## Why Investors Like It
- Higher ARPU Potential – With Pro at $59, you can double or triple ARPU vs. old plan.
- Predictable Upgrade Path – Member cap pushes growth-stage orgs to upgrade naturally.
- Add-On Revenue Streams – Expands LTV without rebuilding the core product.
- Clear Market Segmentation – Serves small, mid, and large orgs without diluting focus.
